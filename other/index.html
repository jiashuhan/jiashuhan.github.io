Side project

N-body gravity simulation

planets is a Python-based n-body simulation of Newtonian gravity. You can set up your own planetary system and choose among several numerical integration methods depending on the accuracy you need.

It started as an undergraduate course project and kept growing. I've used it to check the orbits in the game Kerbal Space Program, and it's the kind of tool I'd like to bring into a computational physics course or an undergraduate research project.
